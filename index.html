shorts-factory

shorts-factory is a private tool its owner uses to publish short videos to their own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram channels (for example Lost Headlines: true stories from public-domain US newspapers, 1850–1925) and to read how those videos perform.

It is not a public service: no one else can sign up or connect an account. It uses the official YouTube, TikTok and Instagram APIs, only for the owner's own accounts.
