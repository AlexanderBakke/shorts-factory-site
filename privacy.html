Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

shorts-factory is a personal tool. It connects only to accounts owned by its operator and is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): the channel's videos and their analytics (views, watch time, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained), and uploading the operator's own videos.
  • TikTok: basic profile info, follower and video counts, the account's public videos and their counts (views, likes, comments, shares), and sending the operator's own videos to the TikTok inbox as drafts.
  • Instagram: the account's Reels and their insights (views, reach, likes, comments, saves, shares, follows, watch time), and publishing the operator's own Reels.

How it is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the operator's videos and to compare how they perform. Access tokens and performance numbers are stored on the operator's own computers and private repository. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about other people is collected.

Third-party terms

By using YouTube features you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok and Instagram data is handled under their own policies.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites, TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Stored data is deleted on request within 30 days: spazzkyshorts@gmail.com.
